# Editorial references (fill these in for your site)

These files are what makes articles specific to your business instead of
generic SEO copy. They ship here as **templates**. Keep your filled-in copies
private: put the skill in your user-level skills folder (for example
`~/.claude/skills/payload-blog-publish/`), not in a public repository, if they
contain internal numbers or customer stories.

## Reading order

1. `voice.md`: who you write for, tone, structure and red lines
2. `facts.md`: the only numbers and facts an article may state about the company
3. `used-keywords.md`: which URL already owns which search intent
4. `stories.md`: approved first-hand stories (at most one per article)

## Conflict rules

- The live site beats these files. If a number here differs from what the site
  publishes (CMS settings, product pages), use the live value and fix this file.
- A number already published keeps its published value. A new article must not
  introduce a second figure for the same fact.
- Anything marked **Needs verification** or **Needs input** stays out of
  published copy until the owner confirms it.
- The red lines in `voice.md` override every SERP finding. A competitor
  covering a topic is not a reason to cross one.
