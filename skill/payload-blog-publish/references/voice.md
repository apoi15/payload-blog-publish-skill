# Voice, structure and red lines

## Reader

<!-- Replace: one paragraph on who reads the blog and what decision they are making. -->
Example: an operations lead at a small company comparing suppliers, reading on
a laptop between meetings. Many readers arrive from AI answer engines
(ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Google AI Mode), so every paragraph has to hold up
when it is quoted alone, out of context.

## Tone

- Plain and specific: numbers, failure points, what you actually check. No
  marketing adjectives, no "in today's fast-paced world" openers.
- A reader should be able to paste any paragraph into an email to their boss.
- "We" is the company. Do not invent people, quotes or named staff.
- Non-default locales are written for their own readers, not translated word
  for word. Every number is identical across locales.

## Structure

1. **Answer first**: 40–70 words that answer the query before any background.
2. **One H2 per reader sub-question**, H3 only inside a section. The page
   renders the H1 from `title`.
3. **Lists and tables for numbers** rather than prose.
4. **One commercial page**, linked naturally with descriptive anchor text.
   Other internal links only where they help the reader.
5. **FAQ**: an H2 followed by H3 questions ending in "?", answered by
   paragraphs. Only People Also Ask questions actually observed.
6. **Close** with one honest takeaway and a soft next step (a `:::cta` block
   or a contact link).

## Evidence rules

- Stock photos illustrate context only. Never caption them as your premises,
  staff, products or customers.
- Customer stories name what you are allowed to name (for example country,
  industry and customer type) and never a customer's brand without written
  permission.
- Competitor pages set intent, format and length. Never copy their sentences,
  tables, images or outline.

## Red lines

<!-- Replace with your own. Examples of the kind of rule that belongs here: -->
- No prices, fees or estimates that are not backed by a real price list.
- No positioning the business has decided against.
- Content already cited by AI answers is extended, not rewritten: add
  sections; do not change existing sentences or numbers unless they are wrong.
- No brand name in `metaTitle` if the layout appends it.
