# Approved first-hand stories

An article may use at most one story, and only one tied to the decision it
helps the reader make. Every story here must be confirmed by the business
owner. Name only what you are allowed to name.

## Needs input

Ask the owner for stories like these before an article needs one:

- A project that went wrong, what was changed and how long it took.
- A first order or first month at the smallest size you sell.
- A quality problem caught before it reached the customer.
